Privacy policy · Hive

Last changed 27 October 2026 (WhatsApp chats downloaded to my laptop)

Who this is about

Hive runs only on my own laptop, and I'm its only user: the owner of the GitHub account Zeldexy. There's no Hive server and no Hive account.

What Hive gets from Google

When I sign in, Google asks me for four permissions:

  • See and download any calendar you can access. Hive reads my calendars and their events to show them next to my other dates.
  • Make secondary Google calendars, and see, create, change and delete events on them. Hive makes its own calendars and keeps the events I accept there.
  • See, create, change, and delete events on Google calendars you own. When I change one of my own Google events in Hive (its name, time, place or details), Hive makes the same change in Google. It doesn't add or delete events in my own calendars, and it never changes calendars that others share with me.
  • Read all resources and their metadata – no write operations (Gmail, read only). Hive looks for dates in my mail: invitations, meeting times and deadlines become proposals that I accept or decline.
    • Of every message, Hive reads only the sender, subject and date, to show me who writes to me.
    • It reads a message in full only when its sender is on a list I keep myself (my university, people I choose). Mail from anyone else is never read beyond those headers.
    • A calendar invite attached to such a message is read on my laptop.
    • The other messages from senders on my list are sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, which I use under my own Claude account, to find the dates in them. It sees the whole message. Model training on my chats is turned off in that account.
    • Hive also reads mail that I send myself to my own Hive address (a plus-address of my Gmail), and only mail that I sent. Such a mail can hold a WhatsApp chat that I exported by hand on my phone and mailed to myself, or a message I pasted into it. Hive never contacts WhatsApp. The new lines of that chat are sent to Claude, as above, to find the dates in them, and only the dates found are kept.
    • Hive never sends, deletes, labels or changes mail.

Hive asks for nothing else: not my contacts, files or profile.

Where the data stays

  • Calendar data is kept in Hive's database on my laptop.
  • From Gmail, Hive keeps only what it found (an event, with the sentence it came from and a link to the message) and a log of which messages it checked (sender, subject, date, what became of it). It doesn't keep the messages' text, and the log stays on my laptop.
  • From an exported WhatsApp chat, Hive keeps only the date it found, the sentence it came from, the chat's name and who wrote it, and a log of which exports it read (chat name, how many lines, from when to when). It doesn't keep the chat's text. The mail with the export stays in my Gmail until I delete it.
  • A WhatsApp chat that I download to my laptop is filed in a folder of Hive on my laptop and read the same way (its new lines go to Claude, as above, and Hive's database keeps only what is listed in the point before). The downloaded file itself stays on my laptop, outside my notes and outside every online backup. Hive never contacts WhatsApp or my phone.
  • The sign-in token Google gives Hive is kept in Windows Credential Manager on my laptop, never in a file.
  • A backup of Hive's database goes to a private GitHub repository that only I can see.
  • Hive sends Google data to no one else, apart from the messages sent to Claude to find dates in them (above): no servers, no advertising, no analytics, no selling. It isn't used to train AI models.
  • When I ask an AI coding assistant to help me with Hive on my laptop, it can see the data there while it helps, like any file I open with it.

Stopping and deleting

  • I can delete Hive's data at any time.
  • To end Hive's access: myaccount.google.com/connections, then Hive, then "Delete all connections". Hive's own sign-out command does the same.
  • Hive's calendars can be deleted in Google Calendar's settings.

Google's rules

Hive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
