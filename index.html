Hive

Hive is a personal organizer that I, the owner of the GitHub account Zeldexy, built for my own studies. It runs only on my laptop and isn't offered to anyone else.

It brings my timetable (TUMonline), my course deadlines (Moodle) and my Google Calendar into one place, where I decide what goes into my calendar. Events I accept go into Hive's own calendars in my Google account, so they show on my phone.

This page exists because Google asks every app that signs in with a Google account for a home page, a privacy policy and terms of service.
